## Why this exists

Most trading content shows the winners. I wanted a record that shows the whole process: the idea, the exact rules,
the costs, the result, and the reasons a result might not hold. Writing it in public keeps me honest.

## Why only CL

Everything here is tested on CL1!, NYMEX WTI crude oil futures. One market means the costs, sessions and data quirks
can be learned properly instead of guessed. Every test uses the full-size CL contract only.

## Who does what

I decide what to test and the rules each test follows. Claude, an AI assistant, builds the tester and the
strategies. Codex, a second AI from a different company, reviews the code and independently rebuilds key results.
A result only counts as replicated when the two engines agree trade for trade.

## Disclaimer

**This is not financial advice.** Everything on this site is backtest research on past data, shared for education
and to document the process.

- Results are **in-sample** unless a page clearly says otherwise. Many are the best of many tries, which makes them
  look better than what the same rules can be expected to earn.
- Backtests leave out real-world effects: changes in liquidity, fills worse than the tested slippage, data errors
  and changes in the market itself.
- Charts that teach how a strategy works use **illustrative prices**, not real market data.
- Past results do not predict future results. Futures trading carries a high risk of loss.
