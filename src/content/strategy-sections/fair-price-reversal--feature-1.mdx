## Test every setting (F1)

This stage runs every combination of four settings (192 in total) for each session over 16 years of 1-minute data,
then selects separate winners for 13 metrics. Use the selector to compare profit, win rate, risk and trade excursions.
The previous net-profit report remains available as a historical reference.
