## The rules

These are the rules behind the saved results. Entries will be updated to limit buy/sell at the planned price;
that strategy change has not been tested yet.

The signal is the indicator's own logic, unchanged. The trade rules are mine, because the indicator only draws
signals and never trades.

- **Fair price.** The body (open to close) of the candle just before the session opens.
- **Setup window.** Starts at the session open. Price must move beyond the body by at least a set multiple of
  ATR(14). ATR is a measure of normal candle size.
- **Long signal.** After a move down, a lower swing high forms. A candle then closes above that swing high while
  still below the body. Shorts are the mirror image. At most one long and one short per session per day.
- **Entry.** A market order at the next candle's open, only when no trade is open.
- **Stop.** The most extreme price of the move away, fixed when the signal fires.
- **Target.** One of four choices, tested as a setting: the near body edge, the far body edge, 1R or 2R. R is
  the distance from the signal close to the stop.
- **Session end.** Any open trade is closed at the flat time: the close order is sent on the candle before, so it
  fills at the flat minute's open (or the next open if that candle is missing).
