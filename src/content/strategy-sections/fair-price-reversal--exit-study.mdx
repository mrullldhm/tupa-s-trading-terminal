## Exit study: breakeven and trailing stop

Here the entry and the first stop stay exactly as F1 found them, and only the exit changes. **T** is the target
distance: how far the profit target sits from the entry. The study tried breakeven by touch, by close and by time;
trailing stops that follow a fraction of T, an ATR line, the last confirmed owner swing, a step ratchet, the best close, an
EMA or Parabolic SAR; combinations with three declared breakevens; and a plain time exit. Stops move at each
1-minute close and work from the next bar.

### What this means

The table shows the regenerated owner-swing study. Only exit management varies; all swing trailing rules
use the same fixed owner swings as entry structure. Each winner is selected in-sample from many trials.
