## Test every setting (F1)

The lowest timeframe allowed to supply the bias is tested at 15 minutes, 1 hour, 4 hours and daily.
Every timeframe uses exactly the fixed owner swing rule. All four configurations run on the available CL
one-minute history, New York session only, with commission and no added slippage. The selector finds separate
winners for 13 metrics.

### What this means

All four configurations lost money in this run. The best net-profit configuration, using the daily as the
lowest eligible bias timeframe, lost $13,547.10 over 855 trades. Its win rate was 31.11%.
