## The idea

The original indicator by LuxAlgo watches one candle: the last 1-minute candle before the US stock market opens at
9:30 ET. Its body is treated as a **fair price**. When the open pushes price far away from that level and then the
move runs out of steam, price often snaps back toward it.

The original was made for Nasdaq futures. Here the same idea runs on **CL1!**, front-month crude oil, at each of the
three CL session opens instead of 9:30 ET. Click through the steps below to see one trade from start to finish.
