Fair Price Reversal and Tupa Trade use only the owner swing definition in `indicators/structure_swings.pine`.
The same rule applies to entry structure, swing trailing, feature searches, validation and charts. Swing
detection is never optimized. A swing confirms at the next candle close, alternates with the opposite type,
and follows the owner's equality, replacement and outside-candle ordering rules.

The regenerated F1 net-profit references, after commission with no added slippage, are:

| Strategy / session | Net profit | Closed trades |
|---|---:|---:|
| Fair Price Reversal / Asia | −$6,418.68 | 1,634 |
| Fair Price Reversal / London | +$11,712.66 | 1,967 |
| Fair Price Reversal / New York | +$60,180.72 | 4,664 |
| Tupa Trade / New York | −$13,547.10 | 855 |

These are in-sample selections. F1 also has separate winners for 13 metrics.

Six F2 searches used 1,500 trials with fixed swings. The three random-filter candidates were validated:
**none advances to F4**. New York passed the procedure checks but missed the deflated Sharpe threshold;
London and Asia also failed the walk-forward profit requirement. The wider searches remain exploratory.

The exit study tested 396 rules per session. Swing trailing used exactly the same owner swings as entry
structure. No exit rule beat the baseline in London or New York; Asia's best exit still lost money.
