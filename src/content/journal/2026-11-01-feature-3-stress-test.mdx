> **Historical record.** Current results use the fixed owner swing rules established on 4 November 2026.
> See the strategy pages for regenerated results; the figures below describe the earlier run.

The first stress tests covered two Fair Price Reversal candidates from F2, in New York and London. They used development data before 2024.

The checks included year-by-year re-selection (walk-forward), how often the search winner fails on another
part of the data (PBO), and whether its Sharpe ratio beats a luck threshold for the number of tries (DSR).
Codex independently checked the saved calculations.

Those results belong to the previous execution assumptions. Selection and stress tests must be run again
after the planned limit-entry updates before a candidate goes on to F4.
