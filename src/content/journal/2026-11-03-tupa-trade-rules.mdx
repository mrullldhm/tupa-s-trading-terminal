The first two strategies came from other people's indicators. This one is mine: the way I trade crude oil by hand,
using only swing highs and lows.

Writing it down was the hard part. I walked through 26 October one timeframe at a time, from the daily down to
1 minute, and answered one question at a time about what I was really doing. Each answer became a rule. Some
answers changed what I thought I did: for example, the 1-hour chart did not need to be in a downtrend for that
short. Only the 15-minute and 5-minute charts did.

The rules ask for an order type the tester did not have: the **stop-limit**. It waits for price to touch the
break point, then sells at a limit price. The tester now supports it, with the rule written down in the engine
document. That rule is not yet checked against TradingView.

What exists now:

- The strategy in code, plus a Pine v6 twin for TradingView.
- A test that checks the code against a second, line-by-line copy of the Pine script on synthetic data. The two
  agree on every trade.
- A test on real data that must reproduce the 26 October trade exactly before any other result counts.

What comes next: the first full run, then Codex's review and replication, as with every strategy. Some rules are
still open, such as how to choose the timeframe being tested when more than one qualifies. The test will try a few
answers, and the results will say they are in-sample and the best of those tries.
