The first strategy through the tester is LuxAlgo's Fair Price Reversal, moved to the three CL session opens.
The test tried 192 settings per session over 16 years of 1-minute data.

The best setting made about +$42,500 in New York, +$34,600 in London and +$1,200 in Asia after commission.
Those are the best of 192 tries on the same data, so they do not establish a tradable edge.

Codex independently rebuilt the New York baseline and matched all 3,710 trades to the cent.

The site now displays baseline results only. Limit-entry strategy updates and new tests are pending.
