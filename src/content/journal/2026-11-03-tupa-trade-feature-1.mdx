> **Historical record.** Current results use the fixed owner swing rules established on 4 November 2026.
> See the strategy pages for regenerated results; the figures below describe the earlier run.

The first check was the example I built the rules from: the 26 October short. The tester took it at the same
minute and price and made the same 25 ticks, and Codex got the same with its own code. So the code does what the
written rules say.

Then the full test: 16 years of 1-minute data, New York session only, four settings for the one open rule (how low
the tested timeframe may go).

**All four lose money, after commission.** The best one lost $14,149 over 858 trades. It won 31% of its trades. With wins about 1.8 times the size of losses, it needed about 36% to break even.
Only 5 of 17 years made money.

Codex's review also found a bug in the Pine version: it never reset the one-trade-per-session rule, so in
TradingView it would have stopped after the first trade. That is fixed. A second finding, about 2010 data with bars
during the daily break, is fixed in the code and waiting for Codex's recheck.

This tests the rules as I wrote them down, not the way I trade by hand. The next step is to put the coded trades
next to how I would have traded the same days and find where they differ.
